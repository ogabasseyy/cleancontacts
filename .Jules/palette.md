## 2026-10-23 - Cohesive screen reader announcements on custom Compose cards
**Learning:** Adding `semantics(mergeDescendants = true)` alone to a parent container (e.g. `Surface`) simply causes screen readers to concatenate inner texts sequentially (e.g., "count, title, description, chevron icon"). This often leads to clunky and disjointed audio feedback for users navigating via keyboard or accessibility services.
**Action:** When grouping child components with `semantics(mergeDescendants = true)`, explicitly synthesize a properly formatted `contentDescription` on the parent modifier to ensure screen readers read a cohesive, structured sentence (e.g., `contentDescription = "$count $title. $description"`). This ensures the context and order of information make sense audibly.
