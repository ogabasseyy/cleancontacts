# Best Contacts Cleaner Apps in 2026: Complete Comparison

Your phone's contact list grows messier every year. Between synced accounts, app imports, and years of accumulated entries, most people have hundreds of duplicate and junk contacts clogging their address book. The right contacts cleaner app can fix this in minutes.

We tested the leading contacts cleaner apps available in 2026 to help you choose the best one for your needs.

## What to Look for in a Contacts Cleaner

Before comparing apps, here's what matters most:

- **Duplicate detection accuracy** — Does it catch near-duplicates or only exact matches?
- **Cross-account support** — Can it merge contacts across Google, iCloud, and device accounts?
- **Junk detection** — Does it identify contacts with missing names, invalid data, or sensitive information?
- **Privacy** — Does the app upload your contacts to external servers, or process everything locally?
- **Undo/backup** — Can you reverse changes if something goes wrong?
- **Batch operations** — Can you fix everything at once, or must you handle contacts one at a time?

### 2. Apple's Built-in Duplicate Detection (iOS only)

**Best for: Quick fixes on iPhone with exact duplicates only**

Since iOS 16, Apple has included basic duplicate detection in the Contacts app. It appears as a "Duplicates Found" banner at the top of your contact list.

**Key features:**
- Detects exact name matches
- One-tap merge per duplicate pair
- Built into iOS — no download needed

**Limitations:**
- Only catches exact matches (misses "Jon" vs "John")
- No cross-account detection
- No batch merge — one pair at a time
- No junk contact detection
- No backup or undo

**Privacy:** On-device processing.

**Price:** Free (built into iOS).

**Platforms:** iOS only.

### 3. Google Contacts Merge (Android & Web)

**Best for: Google account users who want basic cleanup**

Google Contacts includes a "Merge & fix" feature that suggests duplicate merges for contacts stored in your Google account.

**Key features:**
- Suggests merges for similar Google contacts
- Available on web (contacts.google.com) and Android
- Can add missing info and fix formatting

**Limitations:**
- Only works with Google account contacts
- Misses device-stored contacts
- Limited fuzzy matching
- No junk detection beyond missing info
- No undo system

**Privacy:** Contacts are already in Google's cloud.

**Price:** Free.

**Platforms:** Android and Web.
